SMUNOPOLY — RA Training 2026-2027
=================================

HOW TO START IT
---------------
Open the web address for the board in any browser. Bookmark it.

The first time, you'll see two buttons:
   "Start a New Game"          - begins fresh, all teams on GO with $1,500
   "Continue a Saved Game"     - opens a game file you saved earlier

That's it. Nothing to install, no accounts, no passwords.

TIP: for a projector, click the "Full Screen" button in the top right.

NO INTERNET? Double-click index.html and it runs the same way, straight
off the computer. Worth keeping a copy on a flash drive just in case.


THE FOUR GAME PIECES
--------------------
   Silver surfboard      Teal T-Rex
   Orange top hat        Light blue rubber duck

All four start on GO. Each team starts with $1,500.


THE FIVE THINGS YOU'LL DO
-------------------------
1. MOVE A PIECE
   Click the piece. It starts glowing yellow.
   Click the square you want it on. Done.
   (Changed your mind? Press the Esc key.)

2. ADD OR TAKE AWAY MONEY
   Each team has a card on the right side of the screen.
   Type an amount in the little box, then click "+ Add" or "- Take".
   There are also one-click buttons for +$200 GO, +$50 and +$100.

3. PUT A HOTEL ON A SQUARE
   Click the square (not a piece).
   Click the team that won the bid.
   The hotel appears in that team's color.
   To remove it, click the square again and choose "No hotel".

4. CHARGE RENT
   Click the square. The rent is already filled in.
   Click "<team> pays". It takes the money from that team and gives it
   to whoever owns the square automatically. If nobody owns it, the
   money just goes to the bank.
   (You can type a different number first — handy for the Maintenance
   utility square, where rent is the dice roll times 4.)

   There is also a "Pay Rent / Move Money" box at the top of the right
   side for moving cash between any two teams.

5. FIX A MISTAKE
   Click "Undo" at the top. It steps back one change at a time.


SAVING AND CONTINUING
---------------------
The game saves like a Word document: you save it to a file, and you open
that file to pick back up.

TO SAVE, at the end of each day:
   Click "Save Game" (the green button at the top).
   A file lands in your Downloads folder named something like
      smunopoly-2026-08-14-1630.json
   That file IS the game. Keep it somewhere you'll find it. Emailing it
   to yourself or dropping it on a flash drive is a fine idea.

TO PICK BACK UP the next morning:
   Open the website.
   Click "Continue Game".
   Find your smunopoly-....json file and open it.
   The board comes back exactly as you left it.

This works on ANY computer. Save on one laptop, carry the file, open it
on another — the game comes with you.

WATCH THE BADGE at the top of the screen:

   "Game saved"        Your file is up to date.
   "Unsaved changes"   You've moved things since your last save.
                       Click "Save Game" again.

If you try to close the tab with unsaved changes, the browser will stop
and ask you first.

The browser also quietly remembers the game on its own, so accidentally
closing the tab isn't a disaster. Don't lean on it though — school
computers get wiped. The file you download is the real save.

The "Start Over" button wipes the board back to day one. It asks twice,
and it does not touch any file you already saved.
